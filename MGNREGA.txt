    <section id="about" class="container">
        <h3 class="section-title">What is MGNREGA?</h3>
        <div class="grid-2">
            <div>
                <p>Enacted in September 2005, the <strong>Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA)</strong> stands as one of the largest social safety nets globally.</p>
                <br>
                <p>Unlike conventional supply-led employment initiatives, MGNREGA operates on a statutory, demand-driven framework. It guarantees baseline economic security by offering up to 100 days of unskilled manual labor per fiscal year to any rural household with willing adult volunteers.</p>
            </div>
            <div class="gallery-item" style="height: 280px;">

    <!-- Vision & Objectives Section -->
    <section id="vision" style="background-color: #f3ebe1; padding: 1px 0;">
        <div class="container">
            <h3 class="section-title">Vision & Core Objectives</h3>
            <div class="features-grid">
                <div class="card">
                    <h4>Social Protection & Entitlement</h4>
                    <p>Guarantees a statutory legal fallback structure for vulnerable, under-employed communities to counter unexpected systemic fiscal shocks.</p>
                </div>
                <div class="card">
                    <h4>Asset-Driven Rural Growth</h4>
                    <p>Focuses structural output directly on high-yield, durable local resources—including watershed maintenance, canal digging, and rural road networks.</p>
                </div>
                <div class="card">
                    <h4>Decentralized Democratization</h4>
                    <p>Empowers grassroots Planning units through Panchayati Raj Institutions, letting the Gram Sabha directly map local employment needs and shelf-of-works.</p>
                </div>
            </div>
        </div>
    </section>

    <!-- Features Section -->
    <section id="features" class="container">
        <h3 class="section-title">Salient Features of the Scheme</h3>
        <div class="features-grid">
            <div class="card">
                <h4>Legal Enforcement Safeguard</h4>
                <p>Mandates that employment must be granted within 15 days of formal application. If the local state infrastructure delays allocation, an <strong>Unemployment Allowance</strong> becomes legally payable.</p>
            </div>
            <div class="card">
                <h4>Proximity Metrics</h4>
                <p>Guarantees assignments within a 5-kilometer radius of the recipient's home. Cross-boundary exceptions attract a mandatory 10% premium wage supplement.</p>
            </div>
            <div class="card">
                <h4>Gender Equity Mandate</h4>
                <p>Enforces strict gender wage parity alongside a legal requirement allocating at least one-third of all active roles exclusively to women workforce participants.</p>
            </div>
            <div class="card">
                <h4>Accountability Matrix</h4>
                <p>Explicitly bans external commercial contractors and high-displacement heavy machinery to ensure structural expenditure prioritizes manual, human labor.</p>
            </div>
        </div>
    </section>
